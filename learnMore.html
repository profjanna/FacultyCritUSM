I will be starting a super informal art critique group just for university art faculty and staff. No pressure, just a chance to share what we're working on, get some fresh perspectives, and connect with colleagues in a different way. We all struggle to make time for our work during the semester. My goal for this group is to create a supportive space to talk about anything from finished pieces to works in progress. -Asst. Prof Janna Ahrndt
